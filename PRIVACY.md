# Privacy Policy for Booking System

**Last Updated: August 25, 2026**

This Privacy Policy explains how **Booking System** ("we", "our", or "the application"), developed by **Vecvel / besaoct**, handles information when you use our desktop application.

---

## 1. Local Data Storage & Offline-First Design

Booking System is designed with an offline-first architecture:
* **All transactional and business data** (including bookings, ticket details, show timings, movies, seat layouts, taxes, cinema profiles, and audit logs) are stored **exclusively on your local computer** in an embedded SQLite database (`sql.js`).
* We do **NOT** transmit, upload, or sync your cinema or customer data to external servers or third-party cloud services.
* You retain 100% ownership and control over your database files and database backups.

---

## 2. Information We Collect

* **No Personal Data Collection:** The application does not collect, harvest, track, or sell any personal data or usage analytics.
* **No Telemetry or User Tracking:** There are no third-party analytics trackers, advertising SDKs, or background telemetry services bundled within the application.

---

## 3. Hardware & System Permissions

When permitted by the user, the application accesses:
* **Printers & Printing Services:** Required solely to send ticket print commands to connected thermal receipt printers or to render print previews.
* **File System Access:** Required solely when you explicitly choose to export or import database backups (`.sqlite` files) or save Daily Collection Report (DCR) PDF documents.

---

## 4. Third-Party Services

The application does not integrate with third-party tracking or advertising networks.

---

## 5. Security

Because all data is stored locally on your device, the security of your database relies on your operating system's security controls and user account permissions. We recommend keeping regular database backups using the built-in **Audit & Backup** module.
